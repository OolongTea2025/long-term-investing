## Before anything else

When I first wanted to learn about investing, I was like most people: scared by the market, taken in by "stock gurus", and out of pocket for reasons I didn't understand at the time.

Then a friend, **without ever charging me a cent**, quietly sat down and taught me what they knew, from the beginning. They never told me which stock was about to take off. They just patiently explained that investing isn't a coin flip — it's **a skill you can use for the rest of your life without lying awake at night**.

That help changed how I think about money, and about life.

One line has stayed with me: **when someone helps you for free, pass it on.** So I put everything together into this site, **also free**, for the next person standing where I once stood: confused, and unsure whose advice to trust.

If that's you — welcome. Take your time. Nobody here is trying to make money off you.

Two things to say up front. **This site was written by a Hong Konger**, and the original is in Cantonese; this is the English edition. If the phrasing occasionally reads a little unlike a native English writer, I hope you'll bear with it — corrections are genuinely welcome.

The second thing matters more. Everything local — which broker to use, how tax works where you live — is something this site **deliberately will not spell out**. It only tells you that the thing exists, what to search for, and where to go and check. Those rules differ in every country and change constantly, and a stale number does more harm than no number at all. Money amounts are shown in **US dollars purely as illustration**; what matters is the ratio to your own spending, not the currency.

## One promise: this will still be true in ten years

A lot of guides out there tell you **what to buy this month**, or **whether now is a good time to get in**. That kind of thing is useless within a few years.

This site deliberately goes the other way: **it only teaches principles that survive time.**

Index funds, diversification, holding for the long run, keeping your own emotions in check — these were true ten years ago, they're true today, and they'll be true ten years from now. You don't need to come back for updates. Learn it once, use it for life.

## How to use this site

1. **Work out where you are first.** Go to [📍 Find your Level](level.md), find yourself on the ladder, and see **what actually matters at your stage**.
2. **Then read the chapters for your Level.** You don't have to read everything at once — each chapter says which Levels it's for.
3. **Beginners only need the Beginner section to get started.** The intermediate and theory sections are there for when you want depth.
